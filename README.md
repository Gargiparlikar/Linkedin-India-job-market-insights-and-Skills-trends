# 📊 LinkedIn Job Market Intelligence & Skill Trend Analytics

An end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) + Interactive Streamlit Dashboard that analyzes LinkedIn job postings to uncover in-demand skills, hiring trends, experience-level demand, and location-wise insights, with a strong focus on the Indian job market.

# 🚀 Problem Statement

The job market—especially in fast-growing regions like India—is evolving rapidly and increasingly driven by specific technical skills.

Job seekers struggle to identify which skills are truly trending vs emerging

Recruiters lack a clear benchmark of skill demand across locations and experience levels

Raw job posting data is large, cluttered, and unstructured

Static reports fail to capture the dynamic nature of hiring trends

# 👉 There is a strong need for an automated, interactive, and data-driven system that cleans, analyzes, and visualizes job market data to bridge the gap between talent supply and industry demand.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

Clean and structure large-scale LinkedIn job posting data

Identify top trending job roles in India

Analyze skill demand across locations and experience levels

Highlight skill gaps and emerging technologies

Provide an interactive dashboard for real-time exploration

# 🧩 Business Questions Answered

✔ What are the most in-demand skills in the Indian job market?
✔ Which skills are common across Entry-level vs Mid-Senior roles?
✔ Which cities are hiring the most for tech roles?
✔ Which companies are leading recruiters?
✔ What skills should job seekers prioritize learning?
✔ Where do skill gaps exist in the current market?
